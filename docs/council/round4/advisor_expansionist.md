**Expansionist, round 4 on BRD v2.3**

Rounds 1 to 3 are addressed. I found no new contradictions or silent changes to approved decisions (D-094, numbering OFF, web-only, pricing all intact). I found one material gap that others will likely miss.

**Material 1 (Major): no period-start or deadline reminder, so the retention kill rule measures forgetfulness.**
- This is a monthly-cadence product. Its value only compounds if the seller comes back every period.
- A grep for reminder and nudge terms finds nothing in R1. SI-11 defers invoicing reminders. P-5 defers only SMS and WhatsApp reminders.
- The filing home shows deadlines (GF-12), but only inside the app.
- Gate B stops the product if fewer than 3 of 10 file a second period. The "Second period" metric in section 10 needs 5 of 10.
- Pilot sellers are founder-onboarded, so a lapse may mean nothing was prompting them to return, not that demand is weak. That risks killing a viable wedge, or shipping publicly with a churn problem the pilot never exposed.
- Email reminders use SES, which the BRD already has. They add no new service, no WhatsApp and no mobile app, and they keep data in India. They need no change to any approved decision.

**Concrete BRD change.**
- Add requirement GF-32, "Period reminders".
- Acceptance criteria: an email goes to the owner when a period opens and 3 days before the confirmed GSTR-1 deadline. Where the date is unconfirmed, the email says "check the GST portal". It has an unsubscribe link and an audit entry.
- Add it to section 3a as a pilot-entry item.
- Add one metric line: second-period rate counted with and without a reminder.
- Leave SMS and WhatsApp reminders under P-5.

**Not raised.** I am not re-adding any rejected scope (GSTR-2B, Tally export, tiers). The week-16 timing, where three periods are filed in arrears, is a Minor wording point and not material.

Remaining material objections: 1 (no email period or deadline reminder, so the second-period kill rule is confounded)
Would I back this as the final baseline: yes, once GF-32 is added; as written it is one small text edit away.
