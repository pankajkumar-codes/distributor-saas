**Round 3 review of BRD v2.2: Outsider view (small seller and practising CA)**

I read the framing note and the whole BRD. I did not read the chairman's round-2 verdict, which the framing note said to read first.

**Round 2 fixes.** All nine are closed in the text, and I found no silent changes to D-094, numbering OFF, web-only, or pricing.
- Payment versus L-5 is fixed. "Free for everyone until L-5, by any method" appears in section 10, L-5 and 10a, and is consistent.
- Gate A and Gate B have numbers, owners and backups, and the Gate A and B thresholds are testable.
- Section 3a gives the build list in dependency order.
- The 3B promise is reworded honestly: no ITC and no net payable.

**Gates that depend on the outside world.** The CA's signature, interviews, lawyer review, the portal test upload and the payment provider are each a gate with an owner, a week and a stop rule. That is what the definition requires.

**Reading it as a seller or CA, nothing alarming remains.** "We prepare, you file" is stated plainly, and SP-2 plain words cover "held row" and "needs CA check". The payment-after-value order is disclosed before upload. P-2 is honestly flagged as a proposal, and BL-3 still says "no refunds" until the lawyer reviews it, which L-5 requires.

**Minor items (text-only; none changes my vote):**
1. **ON-3** says the turnover band decides "the 30-day IRN limit" and states the e-invoicing threshold as fact. That is a statutory claim with no "CA to confirm" tag, and I suspect the 30-day limit applies at a different band than ₹5 crore (unverified). Tag it "CA to confirm", or delete it, since Kartaa does no e-invoicing.
2. **NF-12** says personal data is deleted "within 30 days, including from backups", but NF-8 keeps backups for 35 days. Reword to "within 30 days, and from backups as they expire (up to 35 days)".
3. **Section 2 and the BRD intro** say "the ready return" and "ready to check and upload". That sits close to the "ready to file" wording that GC-W1 bans. Use "prepared return".
4. **PROPOSAL:** make the authenticator app mandatory for the CA role, because one CA login reaches many businesses. It is optional and off by default today (ON-1). The cost of leaving it is a single CA-account breach exposing many clients, so decide this before CAH-1 ships.
5. **Gate B** says "three monthly periods", so quarterly (QRMP) filers cannot be counted in the accuracy metric. Say so explicitly.

Remaining material objections: 0 (none)
Would I back this as the final baseline: yes
