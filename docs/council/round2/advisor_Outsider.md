**Round 2: The Outsider (small seller and practising CA reading Kartaa's pitch)**

Round 1 is mostly addressed. The gates L-0 to L-6, NF-17, the competitor table and CA-1 are real improvements. I still find five material objections. All can be fixed in the BRD text.

**1. Blocker: hand-collected payment in 10a contradicts L-5, D-074 and BL-3.**
- 10a says to collect UPI or payment-link money from the first three sellers "after L-1 and L-3".
- BL-1 charges "plus 18% GST", L-5 makes our own GST registration a prerequisite only "when charging starts", and BL-3 says the app does not claim to issue GST invoices until D6 is settled.
- A CA would see an unregistered entity collecting GST-inclusive fees.
- Change: 10a collection requires L-5 (D6 settled). Otherwise the first 10 stay free.

**2. Major: the headline overpromises GSTR-3B.**
- The opening paragraph and section 2 say GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B "numbers ready".
- GF-21 to GF-25 deliver only an outward-supply worksheet. It has no ITC, no net payable, and 3.1.1 reads "check on the GST portal".
- A CA will feel misled at the first demo.
- Change: the headline and ON-10 should say "GSTR-1 JSON plus a GSTR-3B sales worksheet. ITC and payment you enter or do on the portal."

**3. Major: Table 13 is a hidden launch dependency.**
- The launch product (R1 export) cannot export any return with data without Table 13 (GF-30, check C-08).
- D-099 is still "proposed". Build #353 is pending.
- Neither appears in section 13's Pankaj items nor in any L-gate. GF-18 depends on a requirement nobody has approved.
- Change: add "approve D-099" to section 13 and "#353 merged" as a precondition of L-2 and L-4.

**4. Major: the kill rules cannot be tested at the dates given.**
- 10a makes the pilot decision in week 4, which is also when the pilot starts.
- But "second period filed", "3 periods of accuracy" and "7 of 10 reach JSON" need 1 to 3 months of filing cycles.
- Change: give each kill rule an evaluation date and an owner, and state which rules are checked at week 4 (interview-based) and which at month 3.

**5. Major: the concierge filing uses real data with no approval gate.**
- Week 1 runs Vaashi's real filed period through the app. Section 1 and NF-14 allow only synthetic or masked data "until real-data use is approved". Nothing lists that approval.
- Change: add the Pankaj item "approve real-data use (where it runs, who sees it, deletion afterwards)" as a prerequisite of L-0.

**Minor, not counted as material:**
- Customer-facing jargon ("held rows", "needs CA check") has no plain-language glossary.
- P-2's "confirmed wrong" does not say who confirms.
- Section 5 still says "no affordable tool", while Repotic and eVanik exist with unknown prices.
- The Zoho row puts a competitor's claim under "verified".

**Remaining material objections: 5** (hand-collected payment against L-5/D6; the 3B "ready" headline; the Table 13 dependency; untimed kill rules; the real-data approval gate)

**Would I back this as the final baseline: no**, until these five are fixed. They are small text changes.
