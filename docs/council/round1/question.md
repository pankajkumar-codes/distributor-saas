# Council question (BRD v2.0, Kartaa, India market)

We are about to treat the attached BRD as the FINAL baseline for Kartaa, a multi-tenant web SaaS for Indian small GST-registered businesses (invoicing + GST return preparation: GSTR-1 JSON, GSTR-3B worksheet; marketplace reports from Amazon/Flipkart/Meesho; seller or CA files on the GST portal, we never file). Founder-owner is Pankaj; there is no pilot customer yet, no real CA engaged, no deployment, payments are "free for now". The product is largely built (R1-R4 web) but unlaunched.

THE QUESTION: Read the BRD at BRD_PATH. Does this product, as specified, win in the Indian small-business market against Vyapar, Zoho Books, ClearTax, Tally, MyBillBook, Busy and others? What must change in the BRD (scope, positioning, pricing, onboarding, trust, GST compliance accuracy, WhatsApp and regional language, offline, distribution/CA channel, support) so a demanding council would have NO remaining material objection?

STAKES: this is the product contract for engineering; wrong positioning or missing table-stakes features cost months. Be specific and honest about market fit; do not flatter.

RULES for your review:
- Cite BRD section numbers or requirement IDs. Rank each finding Blocker / Major / Minor. A finding is "material" only if shipping without addressing it would plausibly lose customers, break trust or compliance, or waste build effort.
- For each finding give a CONCRETE change to the BRD (new/changed requirement or acceptance criterion, or a cut).
- Approved decisions are D-094 India-only data, invoice numbering OFF until CA confirms, web only for R1-R4, and the pricing in section 4. Do NOT silently override them: if you want one changed, label it "PROPOSAL to change an approved decision" with your recommendation and the cost of leaving it.
- Do not invent GST rates, thresholds, deadlines, competitor prices or statistics. Competitor and market facts you are not sure of must be marked "unverified". You may use WebSearch sparingly to check a competitor fact; cite the URL.
- Finish with: "Remaining material objections if all my changes are made: N (list)" and "Would I back this as final: yes/no".
