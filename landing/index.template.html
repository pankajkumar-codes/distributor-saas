What you get

Everything for the GST side of selling, in one place

Invoicing

Create GST sales invoices for your customers, keep your products and stock, and see who owes you what. Taxes are worked out for you from the GSTIN and place of supply.

GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B from your own invoices

At the end of each period, Kartaa prepares your GSTR-1 file and your GSTR-3B figures from the invoices already in it. You review them, then upload or enter them on the GST portal yourself.

Marketplace uploads Add-on

Sell on marketplaces? Upload their sales reports. Kartaa checks every row, flags duplicates and anything unclear, and keeps a record of where each number came from.

Team roles

Invite your accountant, your staff or a partner and give each person only the access they need. Every change is recorded.

Questions

Before you sign up

Does Kartaa file my return for me?

No. Kartaa prepares the GSTR-1 file and the GSTR-3B figures from your invoices. You review them and upload or enter them on the GST portal yourself.

Who is it for?

Small GST-registered businesses: retailers, distributors, wholesalers and online sellers. You need a GSTIN to set up your business.

Do I have to create invoices in Kartaa?

You can start with the GST filing plan and upload your own invoices, or create them in Kartaa with the invoicing plan. Returns are prepared from whichever invoices you have in it.

Can my accountant use it?

Yes. Invite them and choose what they can see and change. Changes are recorded so you can see who did what.

What does it cost?

It is free for now while we launch. Planned prices are shown above, with 18% GST added.
